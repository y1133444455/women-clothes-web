Who We Are

Company Profile

A professional women's wear manufacturer with design, sample-making, mass production and export service all under one roof.

Your Reliable Ladies' Apparel Manufacturing Partner

LunaViva Womenswear Co., Ltd. was founded in 2016 and is located in Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China — a famous home of the Chinese apparel and textile industry with complete fabric, accessory and logistics resources. Our company specializes in the design, production and export of women's fashion apparel, mainly including women's dresses, denim jeans and knitwear (cardigans and sweaters).

We operate a modern garment factory covering about 15,000 m² with more than 200 skilled tailors and pattern-makers, equipped with automatic cutting machines, flat-seam and overlock sewing lines, a knit knitting workshop and a professional garment quality-control room. Our annual output reaches 800,000 pieces. Every style goes through fabric inspection, sample confirmation, in-line quality check and finished-garment inspection before packing.
